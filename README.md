# E-Commerce-Dashboard
An interactive E-Commerce Sales Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, customer insights, product quantities, pricing, and geographical sales trends.

![E-Commerce Dashboard](/images/e-commerce%20dashboard.png)

# INTRODUCTION:

This project is an interactive E-Commerce Sales Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard is designed to transform raw e-commerce sales data into meaningful and easy-to-understand business insights.

It provides an overview of revenue, sales quantity, average unit price, customer spending, monthly sales trends, product/unit performance, and geographical sales. Interactive filters for Country and Year allow users to explore the data dynamically and focus on specific markets or time periods.

The dashboard uses different Power BI visualizations based on the type of information being presented, making the analysis simple, interactive, and visually engaging.

# 📊 Dashboard Visualizations
## 💰 Revenue

Visual: KPI Card with a small trend chart

Shows the total revenue generated along with the year-over-year change.

Why this visual?
A KPI card makes the most important business metric immediately visible. The small trend chart provides a quick view of revenue movement.

## 📦 Quantity

Visual: KPI Card with a sparkline

Shows the total quantity of products sold and its comparison with the previous year.

Why this visual?
The KPI gives a quick summary of sales volume, while the sparkline helps show the overall movement in quantity.

## 💵 Average Unit Price

Visual: KPI Card with a sparkline

Shows the average selling price per unit and its year-over-year change.

Why this visual?
A KPI card clearly highlights the pricing metric, while the trend provides a quick understanding of price movement.

## 👥 Customer Insights

Visual: KPI Card + Donut Chart

The KPI displays the total number of customers, while the donut chart shows the Top 5 spending customers and their contribution to spending.

Why this visual?
The KPI provides a quick customer overview, while the donut chart is suitable for comparing the contribution of a small number of top customers.

## 📈 Monthly Sales Trend

Visual: Line Chart

Shows sales performance from January to December.

Why this visual?
A line chart is well suited for time-based data and makes it easy to identify sales trends, peaks, and declines throughout the year.

## 🏆 Top 5 Units by Quantity Sold

Visual: Horizontal Bar Chart

Displays the top 5 unit types based on the quantity sold.

Why this visual?
Bar charts are effective for ranking and comparing categories, making the highest-volume unit types easy to identify.

## 🌍 Geographical Sales

Visual: Map

Displays sales distribution across different countries and regions.

Why this visual?
A map provides an intuitive geographical view of sales and helps identify important markets and sales locations.

## 🌎 Country Sales

Visual: Horizontal Bar Chart

Compares sales performance across different countries.

Why this visual?
A bar chart makes country-to-country comparisons simple and clearly shows which markets have higher or lower sales.

## 🔎 Country & Year Filters

Visual: Slicers

Interactive slicers are provided for Country and Year.

Why this visual?
Slicers allow users to dynamically filter the dashboard and analyze specific countries or years without modifying the underlying dataset.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Microsoft Power BI
Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
DAX – Measures and calculations
Data Visualization
Interactive Slicers

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to convert raw e-commerce data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that allows users to quickly understand and analyze:

Revenue performance
Sales quantity
Average pricing
Customer spending
Monthly sales trends
Top-performing unit types
Country-wise sales
Geographical sales distribution

The dashboard demonstrates the use of Power BI, DAX, data transformation, and effective data visualization techniques to create a user-friendly analytical report.
